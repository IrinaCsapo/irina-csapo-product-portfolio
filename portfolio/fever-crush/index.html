Fever Crush

Fever Crush is a simplified Match 3 game with a very simple hook: you only have 60 seconds to achieve your best score. The game is simple but incredibly fun to play and can become very addictive, very quickly. After a few rounds, if the game bug got to you, there’s only one way out: crush the germ fever! Approach with caution if you have a lot of work to do.

Crushing Frenzy

T

Fever Crush was created with my partner for fun. It's purposely non-committal and it doesn't require sign-up or next level advancement. You can easily find yourself playing the game three hours later (as I have myself found out), but what can you do when you just have to beat your previous score?! Currently, the game is being redeveloped in Unity to eradicate some browser bugs and make it more responsive but also to introduce some new features and effects.

Go on, have a play, I’d love to know what’s your best score? How about your favourite character? Play the game below and email me at hello@irinacsapo.co.uk with your best result and I’ll tell you mine!
