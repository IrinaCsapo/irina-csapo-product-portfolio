Turning a price increase into a recurring revenue engine

Business Context

Client Website

The subscription price went up and the business had to justify it.

In August 2025, Omaze increased its core subscription price from £10 to £15. To mitigate potential backlash and protect retention, the business introduced a new value proposition: a monthly one million pounds prize experience included "free" within all tier subscriptions.

The product, The Omaze Monthly Millionaire, internally positioned as a free add-on to a monthly subscription-based package, was also available as Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG). The commercial objective was clear: mitigate price increase backlash, increase subscription uptake, improve lifetime value, offset high customer acquisition costs, and generate predictable and stabilised monthly recurring revenue.

This wasn't just a marketing addition. It was a new recurring revenue layer created to meet a potentially sensitve business moment.

The opportunity: Design and optimise a high-acquisition-cost landing page for a new draw experience and turn it into a scalable subscription conversion engine.

The Opportunity

The revenue forecast was £1M+ per month. Initial launch performance came in at ~£650k, a gap that needed closing through design and experimentation. Early on, we identified the challenges that we needed to work on as business, if we wanted to make The Omaze Monthly Millionaire into a successful Omaze product.

The challenge sat at the intersection of two competing pressures. The business needed to protect and grow recurring revenue without cannibalising PAYG sales. Users needed clarity, confidence, and a mobile-first experience that didn't feel like a hard sell.

Challenges

Initial launch performance underdelivered. The first iteration of the page, constrained by pre-existing out-of-the-box Shopify components, generated ~£650k in revenue, significantly below the £1M+ forecast.

A later Meta funnel analysis session with senior PMs highlighted critical mobile behaviour insights: users interact predominantly with the bottom half of mobile screens, and three elements must appear instantly: trust, value clarity, and conversion options.

This reframed the page not as a marketing surface, but as a mobile-first behavioural system that needed immediate redesign.

Testing what actually moves the needle

Hypothesis Framework

If we build trust, reduce uncertainty, and anchor value toward subscription… then we increase subscription conversion and recurring revenue without harming PAYG revenue.

Each experiment was designed around a specific behavioural hypothesis, tested against control, and measured on conversion, revenue, and subscription metrics.

Nudging Behaviour

Users weren't rejecting the price. They were unsure what they were committing to.

Identifying key frictions, I translated high-level behavioural assumptions into modular, testable experiments, each tied to conversion rate, subscription attachment, and revenue per visitor as core success metrics.

This reframed the page not as a marketing surface, but as a mobile-first behavioural system.

The opportunity: Users interact bottom-half first. Add more trust signals. Value must appear instantly. More focus on subscription.

Experiment #1

Solution #1

#1

Building trust

Trust reduces hesitation. Adding third-party validation and authentic testimonials will reduce perceived risk and increase conversion.

Winner: TrustPilot testimonials + rating placed above the fold
Result: +7.3% CVR

Experiment #2

Solution #1

#1

Adding emotion

Emotion drives action. Winner reaction video content above the fold will strengthen emotional salience and increase engagement.

I created an emotional video pieced together from the winner reaction videos available internally and added it as a looped background to the hero.

Winner: Winner reaction video placed above fold
Result: +4.8% CVR

Experiment #3

Solution #1

#1

Value framing

Subscription must feel superior, not secondary. Reframing payment options to emphasise entry volume over price will shift cognitive anchoring toward value.

Winner: Surface subscription early. Visually prioritise subscription card with "Best Value" positioning.
Result: +8.5% CVR · No PAYG cannibalisation

Personal Insights

What this project taught me

Creating a new draw experience 0 to 1, from branding and to conversion optimisation, shifted how I think about product design: from crafting interfaces to engineering conversion systems. It challenged me to balance commercial pressure with user experience, and to prove that design decisions can directly move revenue metrics.

Working within Shopify constraints while running structured A/B experiments taught me to be scrappy without sacrificing strategic rigour. And ultimately, it reinforced my belief that the best product design isn't just usable, it's measurably impactful.
